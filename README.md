# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple program that calculates simple interest on a loan or investment.

## Formula

```
Simple Interest = (P × R × T) / 100
```

- **P** = Principal (the initial amount)
- **R** = Annual interest rate (in %)
- **T** = Time (in years)

## Example

| Principal | Rate | Time | Simple Interest |
|----------:|-----:|-----:|----------------:|
| 1000      | 5%   | 2    | 100             |

## How to use

1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual interest rate.
3. Enter the time in years.
4. The calculator displays the simple interest and the total amount (principal + interest).
